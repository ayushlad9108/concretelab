Six component inputs by experiment

These values are generated only after uploading the component and Lab K workbooks. The classic A-14 matrix sizes are 6 components × 21 experiments (S is 7 × 21 including totals), Z is 63 × 21, and CC matrices are 63 × 63.

Each line represents one component; the horizontal axis is the uploaded experiment/specimen ID. Values are taken from the uploaded workbook, not preset data.

Upload both workbooks and calculate before downloading. The full A-14 polynomial matrices require exactly six parameters; if the upload has a different parameter count, the downloads will explain the supported dimensions rather than silently export incorrect matrices.

Matrix diagnostics

CC = Z·Zᵀ. It is symmetric by construction. CC is not generally the identity; CC·CC⁺ is a projection when rank-deficient.
